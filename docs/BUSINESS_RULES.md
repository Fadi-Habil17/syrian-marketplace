# Business rules and explicit defaults

Authority: `MARKETPLACE_REQUIREMENTS.md` revision 1.2 (sections cited below), the owner's stack instruction, and the B4F submission guide. **Required** means an explicit policy. **Default** means an implementation decision filling a gap, changeable only without overriding a required policy. These are the governing policies; actual backend coverage and test limits are recorded in BACKEND_IMPLEMENTATION_REPORT.md.

## Scope and identity (requirements 1-3, 9)

**Required:** nationwide Syria; Freelance, Professionals, Home Store; responsive web and iOS/Android; shared REST/WebSocket contracts; complete admin actions on both clients; Arabic RTL/English LTR and bilingual listings/storefronts. Store seller delivery and platform drivers are both in release one; assigning a platform driver is optional per order.

**Required:** registration as customer or one business role: Freelancer, Professional, Store Owner, Delivery Driver. Every business role retains customer capabilities. Admin moderates profiles, listings, storefronts/products, restricts/suspends accounts, manages commission and disputes, and accesses audit history.

**Default D01 - sign-in:** normalized unique email/password; bcrypt cost 12, password at least 12 characters and at most 72 UTF-8 bytes, no silent truncation. Owner override (2026-11-24): email verification is removed. New and existing accounts can sign in and use permitted business/payment actions immediately; password-reset emails remain supported. Do not claim verified government identity. Admin is provisioned out of band, never self-selected. Require credential reauthentication for sensitive admin actions; MFA is a later hardening item.

**Default D02 - workspace switching:** serialize on the user. Count ALL their buyer, provider/seller, and assigned-driver commitments: every nonterminal order, any open dispute/revision/cancellation case, any active pending/picked-up delivery, and any nonzero order escrow liability or unpaid settlement obligation. Terminal means completed, cancelled, rejected, expired, or refunded, provided no open case/financial obligation remains. A draft abandoned before agreement/hold is not an active order. Available wallet credit alone is not unsettled escrow. A new switch checks all conditions again and archives the outgoing business profile atomically. Preserve identity, purchases, completed orders, reviews, audit and historical profile references. Reactivation reuses the same archived profile ID and checks the same eligibility; suspension/moderation restrictions survive. At most one ACTIVE business profile per user using a partial unique index. Archive removes owned offers/storefronts from discovery and new orders while preserving historical snapshots; reactivation requires revalidation before republishing and never clears moderation decisions. Driver status is a business workspace for switching purposes.

**Default D03 - restrictions:** suspension denies new writes, login refresh and socket access; funds remain held. Restricted users retain read-only access to their history and a controlled dispute/evidence channel so moderation cannot force forfeiture. Explicit admin case actions remain audited.

## Catalog and geography (requirements 4-6, 9, 11)

**Required:** freelance title/bio/skills/languages/portfolio; bilingual listing title/description, category, delivery time and fixed SYP/USD price; provider create/edit/deactivate. Filters: category, price, delivery time, rating. Professional trade/experience/location/service area/portfolio and fixed-price packages. Store bilingual name/description, logo, governorate/city; bilingual products with category/photos/stock/SYP or USD price; catalog/storefront category/location filtering.

**Default D04 - language and catalog:** both Arabic and English names/descriptions required to publish a listing, package, product or storefront. Drafts may be incomplete. Profile biography/portfolio captions can use either language. Deactivate rather than delete records referenced by orders; snapshot price, language fields, scope, delivery terms and contact/address details at agreement. No cross-seller or cross-currency checkout, discounts, taxes, exchange conversion, or paid scope add-ons in this release.

**Default D05 - geography:** one relational hierarchy Country(SY) -> Governorate -> City/District -> Neighborhood/Area. Use ISO 3166-2:SY codes for the 14 governorates with reviewed Arabic/English names; city/area records are curated local seed data, not a claim of authoritative boundary coverage. Cite and license the actual dataset before importing it. No external geocoder dependency. Profile, address, service-area and driver zones reference this hierarchy; a child must belong to its selected parent. A seller selects supported cities, optionally narrowed to areas; addresses outside them cannot check out. Driver jobs require an assigned city, or governorate-wide coverage explicitly configured by an admin, and an active eligible driver profile.

**Default D06 - scheduling:** Asia/Damascus display; UTC instants for persistence; 30-minute start-time slots, package duration in multiples of 30 minutes. Prevent provider overlap using a PostgreSQL exclusion constraint on reserved time ranges. Proposals do not reserve time until accepted by both parties; acceptance reserves through the 48-hour deposit deadline. An inspection appointment is free in this MVP and creates no separate hold. Unanswered initial requests, counter-slot proposals and sent quotes expire 72 hours after the latest actionable request; inspections cannot silently extend the deadline. Explicitly agreed inspection/reschedule creates a new recorded response deadline and notifies both parties.

## Service agreement, execution and cancellation (requirements 4-5, 7-8)

**Required:** a freelancer and buyer establish scope and fixed total. Professional fixed-price booking needs provider acceptance and schedule confirmation before deposit; buyer must accept a proposed alternate slot. Inspection request contains description, photos and location; provider sends an itemized quote; buyer approval fixes the price before deposit. Rejected booking/quote closes free. Unanswered booking/quote expires in 72 hours. Accepted service agreement has 48 hours to pay the deposit, then closes free and releases time.

**Required:** deposit held -> work -> documented ready -> remaining payment held -> final delivery -> buyer confirmation/dispute/automatic completion. Professional progress includes en route and on site. Freelance milestones record progress only. Final files stay inaccessible until the second hold. A seller submits digital deliverables or records on-site handover. Final handover must follow successful balance funding.

**Default D07 - readiness and deadlines:** store an agreed dueAt at agreement; ready evidence includes scope checklist and nonfinal preview or work record, without granting final-file access. Start the 72-hour balance-payment deadline once readiness is documented; pending-balance reminders at +24h and +48h have unique keys. On expiry use FINAL_PAYMENT_OVERDUE, retain deposit and gating, and create one admin case if none exists. Retry jobs do not reset timers. Missing the balance never charges more or forfeits deposit. An overdue buyer can still fund the balance only if the handling admin authorizes resumption and resolves/freezes the case consistently.

**Required:** either party may cancel before work begins for a full deposit refund and slot release. After work starts buyer cancellation freezes held funds; both parties must accept a documented refund split. Rejection or no agreement in 72h goes to admin. Provider cancellation refunds held funds unless an existing dispute/accepted settlement governs it. Missed due date offers extension or cancellation; contested work/delay claims freeze funds for admin. Buyer delivery dispute/change request suspends completion and freezes funds; mutual revision plan returns to work, with a fresh 72h review after resubmission.

**Default D08 - revision:** no automatic additional charge or revision limit; plan records scope, deadline and both approvals. Fully funded revised work stays fully funded and cannot request a second balance hold. Preserve all submission versions. Any contested revision goes to admin. An accepted cancellation split is finalized with the same conservation/commission checks as an admin decision. Case review timers never auto-award money.

## Store, stock and delivery (requirements 6, 8)

**Required:** quote a serviceable address, delivery options, fee, currency, total and promised terms before funding. Hold all items plus delivery at checkout and atomically reserve stock. Seller accepts/prepares -> ready -> out for delivery -> delivered -> completed. Buyer cancellation before acceptance, seller rejection, or no seller acceptance within 24h refunds items and delivery in full and releases stock. Payment failure advances neither order nor stock reservation. After acceptance buyer cancellation is a request with mutual split or admin review; seller cancellation/seller-caused nondelivery defaults to full refund unless existing dispute governs.

**Default D09 - delivery rates:** admin maintains versioned rates by seller service city, selected method and currency; seller selects supported methods. Store checkout snapshots the quoted rate/terms. No floating distance pricing. A platform request can be recorded at checkout and offered to drivers when ready; seller may request at readiness. An offer that changes total, method or promised terms requires explicit buyer approval BEFORE adjustment; never silently debit a wallet. Any newly approved higher total uses an explicit additional hold with a new idempotency key; lower total uses an explicit refund, while preserving commission snapshot and audit. If adjustment fails retain original terms. This is a store delivery adjustment, never an extra service installment.

**Required:** eligible drivers see only jobs in their assigned city/governorate; one active assignment per delivery; accept, pickup, drop-off history and proof; designated fee paid on completion. If unavailable, offer seller delivery under agreed terms, a buyer-approved reschedule, or full refund if fulfillment unavailable. Return reusable stock on cancellation and record non-restockable quantities.

**Default D10 - dispatch and proof:** unassigned ready delivery escalates to seller after 2 hours; no auto-cancellation based only on driver availability. Provider must record the selected fallback. Reassignment before pickup releases the old assignment with a reason; after pickup requires admin custody review. Buyer approves materially changed terms. Pickup/drop-off events include actor, UTC time and proof attachment reference; do not collect continuous GPS in MVP. Seller delivery has equivalent drop-off proof. A buyer's explicit confirmation can settle an unproven physical delivery; without it auto-settlement is blocked and an admin review case is created at 72h. Admin must adjudicate rather than fabricate proof. Cancelled inventory reconciliation records returned and non-restockable units with reason; total reconciled cannot exceed reserved units.

## Money, escrow and commission (requirements 7-8)

**Required:** local simulator behind `PaymentGatewayProvider`; simulate balances, insufficient funds, successes/failures, holds, releases, refunds, payouts and references. Label simulator UI/API; no live funds or official ShamCash Sandbox. Each wallet/order has one currency, USD cents or SYP pounds. No negative available balance.

**Default D11 - arithmetic:** PostgreSQL BIGINT, TypeScript bigint, JSON decimal strings; positive total bounded at 9,000,000,000,000 stored units for safe external tooling. For positive n/d, half-up is floor((2n+d)/(2d)). Service deposit = halfUp(total/3), balance = total - deposit; reject if either is zero (minimum total 2 stored units). Examples: 100 USD cents => 33 + 67; 101 SYP => 34 + 67; 2 SYP => 1 + 1. Quotes sum item quantity * unit amount exactly; reject mixed currency, negative components or a client-supplied inconsistent total. There are no milestone holds.

**Required:** global commission begins at 0%; optional workspace override including 0%; snapshot first successful hold. Apply only to seller-awarded service/items, excluding refunds and delivery; round once half-up. **Default D12:** represent percentages as integer basis points 0..10000; use null to mean inherit, never truthiness. No commission on free requests, gateway errors, refunds or the delivery allocation. Changing an admin rate cannot change existing order snapshots, even when a later store delivery adjustment is accepted.

**Required:** release only upon eligible completion or documented resolution. Both parties can dispute after any hold and before settlement. Delivery review runs 72h from recorded delivery server time. Dispute freezes funds. Before auto-release recheck fully funded, delivery evidence/explicit confirmation, time, dispute/revision/paused status, and settlement uniqueness IN the settlement transaction. A dispute accepted before settlement commits prevents settlement. Completed orders may open support cases but original payout cannot silently replay/reverse.

**Default D13 - ledger:** double-entry per currency; wallets have available accounts, order escrow liability accounts, platform simulator clearing and commission accounts. Top-ups are simulated and admin-authorized in test mode, from clearing with explicit references, never production funding. Hold moves available -> escrow, payout/refund moves escrow -> destination. Journal debits equal credits per currency; immutable entries, operation unique keys and serializable retries/ordered row locks. Insufficient funds/failed provider simulation creates a failed attempt/audit without financial postings or order advancement. Store reservations, outbox events and audit join the same successful hold transaction. No external network side effect within the local simulator transaction.

Settlement conservation for **held** amount H: buyer refund + seller service/item net + seller delivery award + driver payout + commission = H. Commission = halfUp(seller awarded eligible amount * snapshotted basis points / 10000). Seller net = eligible award - commission. Seller delivery is paid to seller for seller delivery; platform driver fee is paid to the assigned driver on completed documented delivery. For failed/disputed delivery admin decides delivery allocation; seller/driver-attributable failure defaults to refunding delivery fee. Any exception records substantiated evidence and a reason. Never allocate an unfunded remainder. Accepted awards cannot exceed held units. Each settlement operation has exactly one ledger journal.

## Disputes, chat, reviews, files and notifications (requirements 7-9)

**Required:** notify both parties and give each 72h to submit evidence. Authorized handling admins see timeline/chat/quotes/payment references/proof; record decision reason and award amounts, substantiate seller work against agreed scope, refund unused funds. **Default D14:** evidence deadline starts when the case opens for each notified party; failed push does not change server deadline; late evidence requires admin reason. Deadline triggers review, never default forfeiture. Existing disputes govern cancellation rather than a competing refund path; one unresolved financial case per order, with multiple evidence submissions.

**Required:** private order chat, text/images/files, immutable audit; buyer, seller, assigned driver when applicable, handling admin only. **Default D15:** buyers/providers see full shared order conversation; driver sees a separate delivery thread plus minimum contact/address, delivery fee and delivery-proof attachments while assigned. No access to service files, quote attachments, unrelated history, or private evidence. On reassignment revoke subscriptions/download grants immediately. File access is classified: public catalog, order-shared, gated final deliverable, delivery-only, case evidence. Unassigned admins must explicitly take case assignment with audited reason before chat/evidence access.

**Default D16 - reviews:** one buyer review per completed order, 1-5 integer stars; optional text; may edit once within seven days of first publication with immutable versions. No review of cancelled/refunded orders, own services or driver-only assignments. Admin moderation may hide with a reason but cannot rewrite text or ratings. Historical reviews remain tied to archived provider/store profiles.

**Default D17 - uploads:** private S3-compatible storage (authenticated SeaweedFS in current local development; the referenced MinIO image was unavailable); catalog public derivatives only after validation. Images JPEG/PNG/WebP up to 10 MiB; PDF up to 20 MiB; ZIP final deliverables up to 50 MiB (compressed); expanded ZIP limits: 50 MiB per entry, 100 MiB total, 1,000 entries, stored/deflated only, no encryption or symbolic links, validate streamed sizes/checksums without filesystem extraction. These are resource defaults, not a changed upload allowance; max 10 files per message/evidence submission, portfolio 20 files, product 10 images. Reject SVG/HTML/executables, misleading signatures, path traversal and archive extraction. Quarantine until scan succeeds. Private downloads stream through the backend, rechecking current participation/funding/assignment on each request; any download ticket expires within 60 seconds and is reauthorized on redemption. Never expose a direct private object-storage URL. Public catalog derivatives may use short-lived signed URLs. Use content-disposition attachment for files. Gated finals remain inaccessible before full funding. Size/extension/declared MIME/magic bytes and resource owner checks all enforced server-side. Default retention: financial/audit records 5 years; chat/proof/attachments 1 year after closure, longer under open case/legal hold; tombstone removals and never erase audit references. This is a project operational default, not a statement of law.

**Required:** actionable order/payment/chat notifications in app; mobile push for these categories. **Default D18:** transactional outbox -> stored notification -> WebSocket, plus Expo push; user permission and device token registration on mobile, generic lock-screen payloads without file/chat content. Web uses authenticated in-app and WebSocket delivery; browser push, SMS and email marketing excluded. Mobile denial of OS push still leaves persistent in-app notices. Unread state syncs via REST cursor; push/websocket delivery is at least once, deduplicated by event ID. Admin actions/disputes notify affected parties. Terms, amount at risk and server deadlines shown before every hold.

## Default operational targets

**D19:** 95th percentile ordinary API reads <500 ms locally with 50 concurrent demo users; listing pagination <=100 records; all input mutations rate limited by actor/IP, financial commands separately. API liveness is not database readiness. UTC clock injectable for tests; worker polls every 30s, handles due jobs using locks and durable deadlines; late polling never makes an ineligible order eligible. PostgreSQL outbox avoids a new mandatory broker. Daily database backups and private object backups with a demonstrated restore before deployment; structured request IDs, redacted logs and metrics for held balance, overdue jobs, failed push, rejected settlement. These are acceptance targets to measure, not achieved performance claims.

## Additional technical defaults for the database/contract increment

**D20 - store preview:** an unfunded DRAFT preview expires after 15 minutes, with no stock reservation or hold. The buyer sees the authoritative total/options/address/fee first; checkout validates its version and atomically funds/reserves. This does not replace the required full checkout hold or change any 24/48/72h policy.

**D21 - explicit publishing:** valid price/category/type are required for a catalog draft; language fields may initially be empty. Publish commands validate both languages, owner verification/active workspace and clean assets. PATCH merges supplied fields only and never changes existing order snapshots. These are technical catalog/contract defaults, not new payment policies.

## Backend technical clarifications

The backend preserves these commercial rules. The implementation report records technical contract gaps resolved during delivery: owner-only staged inspection photos, nullable unapproved quote totals/due dates, typed moderation content, scoped one-use socket tickets, immutable stock-return proposals and a separately referenced administrative payment-resumption anchor. None introduces another service installment or real payment.

D03 uses password-authenticated, history-only access for suspended accounts; refresh, ordinary writes, administrative powers and sockets remain blocked. Controlled evidence/support/proposal recording remains possible, with restricted funds requiring handling-admin review. Profile suspension also freezes its unsettled business orders. D10's initial delivery promise is 48 hours from preview, snapshotted with the server rate; quote lifetime remains 15 minutes. A resumption deadline is the exact mutually approved future time, at most 72 hours after administrative authorization, without rewriting original readiness.
